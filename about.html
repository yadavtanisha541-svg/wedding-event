About Riwaayat Events & Weddings

A decade of transforming celebrations into unrepeatable royal masterpieces across palaces, estates, and private islands.

Turning Your Vision Into a Celebration

Every love story is unique, and your wedding deserves to be an exquisite, unrepeatable masterpiece.

Founded over 10 years ago in the royal heart of Udaipur, Riwaayat Events & Weddings was born from a singular belief: weddings should feel like timeless poetry brought to life through architectural majesty, heartfelt hospitality, and effortless joy.

We handle every aspect from initial venue scouting, thematic decor sketches, and floral procurement to airport concierge fleets, catering curation, and minute-by-minute ceremony execution. With over 500+ weddings celebrated across Rajasthan, Goa, Mumbai, and international destinations, our reputation is built on trust, discretion, and creative excellence.
